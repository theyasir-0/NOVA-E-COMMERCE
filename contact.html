FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Instant Answers

How long does domestic shipping take?
Express orders placed before 3:00 PM are dispatched on the same business day. Delivery across major metro cities takes 24–48 hours, while standard delivery to other regions takes 2–4 business days. All orders above ₹999 qualify for 100% Free Express Shipping.
How do I apply coupon discount codes like NOVA20?
Open your Shopping Bag drawer by clicking the "Cart" button in the top navigation. In the summary section, type NOVA20 into the coupon input and click "Apply". An instant 20% deduction will reflect on your order subtotal.
What is your 30-day money back guarantee?
If you are not 100% satisfied with your purchase, you may initiate a return within 30 days of receiving your package. Items must remain in their original packaging with warranty seals intact. Refunds are processed back to your original payment method within 3 business days.
Are all products authentic and covered under warranty?
Every single product on NOVA is 100% brand authentic and sourced directly through authorized OEM distributors (Apple, Samsung, Sony, Nike, Dell, etc.). All electronics come with standard 1–2 year brand manufacturer warranties.
